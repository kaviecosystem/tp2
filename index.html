How it works

i

ii

Wait for 2×

Rewards unlock only while KAVI trades at or above twice your entry price.

iii

iv

Each release is 1% of your basis, sold to USDT at market. Total reward caps at 2× your basis.

Understand the risk before investing

Rewards are funded by the pool's reserve and are only guaranteed while it holds enough KAVI. The price gate reads a live on-chain price. Every claim sells KAVI into the market, which pushes the price down. Investments are locked until their price target is met, and if it never is, they stay locked. So invest only if you believe KAVI can reach 2× your entry price.
