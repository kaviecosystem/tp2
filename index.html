Understand the risk before depositing

Rewards are funded by the pool's reserve and are only guaranteed while it holds enough KAVI. The price gate reads a live on-chain price. Every claim sells KAVI into the market, which pushes the price down. Deposits are locked until their price target is met, and if it never is, they stay locked. So deposit only if you believe KAVI can reach 2× your deposit price.
